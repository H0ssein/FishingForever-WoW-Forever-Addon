# FishingForever

**FishingForever** is a lightweight, quality-of-life fishing addon for WoW Forever. It streamlines casting, managing lures, and alt-tabbing so you never miss a catch!

## Features

- **Double-Right-Click to Fish**: Forget about dragging the Fishing skill to your action bars! Simply double-right-click anywhere while you have a fishing pole equipped to cast your line.
- **Smart Lure Menu**: When you don't have a lure applied, the addon automatically detects all fishing lures in your bags and presents a beautifully styled, modern interactive menu right at your cursor.
  - Click any lure in the menu to instantly apply it to your pole.
  - Don't want to use a lure right now? Click the "Ignore" (cross) button to hide the menu and fish without lures for the next 5 minutes.
- **Background Sound Automation**: Automatically enables background sound while fishing (perfect for alt-tabbing), and restores your previous setting when you stop.

## Usage
- Equip a fishing pole.
- **Double Right-Click** to start fishing.
- If a lure is required, select one from the popup menu.
- **Options Panel**: Configure the addon settings from the WoW AddOns Options panel.
